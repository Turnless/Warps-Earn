Introducing WarpsEarn <a href="http://t.me/warpsearn_bot/app">Warps Earn</a>

WarpsEarn is a Telegram Mini App that connects real users to Web3 and Web2 projects that need genuine engagement. 

Instead of projects wasting money on bot farms and fake clicks, they list tasks on WarpsEarn things like following a page, joining a community, or trying out a dApp.

Users on the platform complete those tasks and earn real rewards in return. Every action is verified through built-in security so only human, legitimate activity gets counted. No bots, no ghost accounts, no inflated numbers that mean nothing.

For users, it's a way to earn by actually paying attention to projects worth knowing about. For founders and brands, it's direct access to a real, engaged audience without the usual Web3 marketing waste. There's also a referral system baked in, so users and promoters grow with the platform as it scales. 

It's essentially closing the gap between projects that need visibility and people who are willing to give genuine attention and make sure both sides actually benefit.
